Car rental at Valencia Airport (VLC)

Valencia Airport has a straightforward terminal setup with many rental desks on Floor 0 and vehicle collection in Parking P1. There are also nearby shuttle operators. Compare the convenience of an in-terminal desk with the total price and conditions of off-airport options before choosing.

Terminal desk or shuttle at Valencia Airport?

Terminal desks are attractive when you value the fastest possible collection after landing. A shuttle operator can still be competitive when the savings or rental conditions are better enough to justify the short transfer.

If most of your trip is inside central Valencia, calculate parking and the days you really need a car. If you are heading to beaches, inland towns, events or a multi-stop route, collecting at the airport can be much more convenient.

Four checks before you collect the keys

  1. 1
    Compare total pickup time, not only rental price

    A cheaper off-airport rate may still be a good deal, but include shuttle and check-in time in your decision.

  2. 2
    Confirm the card deposit before travel

    The amount blocked on your card can be more important than a small difference in daily price.

  3. 3
    Save your P1 or shuttle return route

    Airport returns are easier when the exact lane and parking area are saved in advance.

  4. 4
    Check your hotel parking

    Central Valencia accommodation may have limited or paid parking.

Valencia Airport FAQ

Questions travellers ask before booking

Which car rental companies have desks at Valencia Airport?

Aena currently lists Avis, Enterprise, Europcar, Goldcar/InterRent, Hertz, OK Mobility, Sicily by Car and Sixt at the terminal.

Where do I collect an in-terminal rental car at VLC?

Aena lists many desks on Floor 0 and vehicle collection for several companies in Parking P1. Follow your supplier’s voucher for the exact bay.

Does Centauro use a shuttle at Valencia Airport?

Yes. Centauro’s current Valencia Airport instructions direct customers to a meeting point near long-stay Parking P6 for its shuttle service.

Can I use a debit card?

Possibly, depending on the supplier, vehicle category and coverage. Verify the collection-card rules for your exact booking.

Do I need a car for Valencia city?

Not necessarily for a city-only stay. A rental becomes more useful for beaches, inland destinations or a wider road trip.
