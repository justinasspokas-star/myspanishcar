Driving from Seville Airport

Seville Airport is a practical starting point if your itinerary includes the city plus other parts of Andalusia. A rental car is especially useful for multi-stop trips where public transport would require repeated connections. If your route enters Portugal, verify cross-border permission with the supplier before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Seville Airport car rental FAQ

Which car rental companies are inside Seville Airport?

Aena currently lists Avis, Enterprise Rent-A-Car, Europcar, Goldcar-InterRent, Hertz, OK Mobility and Sixt on Floor 0 of the terminal.

Are there shuttle car rental companies at Seville Airport?

Yes. Wiber publishes a nearby Seville Airport office around five minutes away and provides a free shuttle from the terminal.

Can I rent at Seville Airport without a credit card?

Sometimes. Some suppliers accept eligible debit cards, but the exact rule depends on the supplier, vehicle group, rate and deposit. Check the collection conditions for your booking.

Can I drive a Seville rental car to Portugal?

Only if your supplier and tariff allow it. Cross-border permission and fees vary, so confirm this before booking rather than at the desk.
