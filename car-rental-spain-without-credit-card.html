Car rental in Spain without a credit card

You do not always need a credit card to rent a car in Spain. Several suppliers publish debit-card options, but the deposit, physical-card and vehicle-category rules still matter. Compare those conditions before you choose the cheapest price.

Quick answer

Can you rent a car in Spain without a credit card?

Yes, with selected suppliers and vehicle groups. The key is to separate three questions: whether a debit card is accepted, whether a deposit is required, and whether your chosen vehicle category has extra restrictions.

What “without a credit card” actually means

1

Debit accepted

The company allows an eligible debit card at pickup.

2

Deposit may remain

A debit card can be accepted while a security amount is still charged or held.

3

Physical card

Some suppliers require the actual bank card, not a virtual wallet.

4

Vehicle limits

Luxury and high-value groups can require stricter payment methods.

Checklist if you travel without a credit card

  • The exact pickup office accepts your debit card
  • Your card brand and type are eligible
  • The card is physical if the supplier requires it
  • The card is in the required person's name
  • You know the deposit or security amount
  • Your bank balance can cover rental + deposit
  • Your vehicle group has no extra credit-card requirement

No credit card vs no deposit: they are different

A rental can accept a debit card and still require a large security amount. Conversely, some all-inclusive or premium tariffs advertise no-deposit options. Compare the payment card and the deposit separately.

Frequently asked questions

Car rental Spain without credit card FAQ

Can I rent a car in Spain without a credit card?

Yes, some suppliers in Spain accept eligible debit cards, but rules vary by rental company, vehicle category, station and tariff. You must confirm the pickup conditions for the exact booking.

Which companies let me rent without a credit card in Spain?

Wiber and SIXT publish debit-card options in Spain. Europcar also publishes debit-card acceptance in Spain, with restrictions, while OK Mobility states that customers who only have a debit card need an eligible Premium Cover option.

Does renting without a credit card mean there is no deposit?

No. A debit-card rental can still require a deposit or security amount. Some no-deposit tariffs exist, but they are specific products and conditions can still apply.

Can I use Apple Pay or a virtual card instead?

Do not assume so. Some suppliers require a physical bank card at pickup. Wiber, for example, says it does not accept virtual/digital cards or smartphone payments.

Can I rent a premium car without a credit card?

Premium and luxury categories often have stricter card requirements. Check the exact vehicle category before booking.
