The real comparison is therefore less about finding the desk and more about the total rental conditions—deposit, accepted card, excess, fuel policy and return rules.

When renting at Alicante Airport makes sense

A rental car is especially useful when your trip includes several Costa Blanca stops rather than only central Alicante. Routes towards Benidorm, Altea, Calpe, Jávea or inland villages become much easier when you are not tied to bus timetables.

If you only plan to stay in Alicante city, compare the cost of parking and the number of days you actually need a car. For a split trip, collecting the car later can sometimes be cleaner than keeping it unused in the city.

Four checks before you collect the keys

  1. 1
    Match the card to the main driver

    Some tariffs accept debit cards while others require a credit card in the main driver’s name.

  2. 2
    Treat the deposit separately from the rental price

    A low headline rate can still require a significant temporary card hold.

  3. 3
    Confirm the return fuel rule

    Full-to-full is easy to compare, but always read the exact wording on the voucher.

  4. 4
    Save your flight and desk details offline

    A single terminal simplifies pickup, but late arrivals and desk opening hours still matter.

Alicante Airport FAQ

Questions travellers ask before booking

Which car rental companies are inside Alicante Airport?

Aena currently lists Avis, Centauro, Europcar, Goldcar/InterRent, Hertz/Firefly, OK Mobility, Record Go and Sixt on Floor 0 in Arrivals.

Is Centauro inside Alicante Airport?

Yes. Aena currently lists Centauro on Floor 0 in Arrivals, and Centauro’s own Alicante Airport page also instructs customers to go to its desk in the arrivals hall.

Can I rent at Alicante Airport with a debit card?

Sometimes. Card acceptance depends on the supplier, vehicle group and coverage. Check the collection-card rules for your exact rate, not only the online payment method.

Do I need a rental car for Alicante city?

Not necessarily. A car becomes more valuable if your itinerary includes several Costa Blanca resorts, beaches or inland towns.

Should I book Alicante Airport car hire in advance?

For peak holiday periods, advance booking gives you a better chance of choosing the vehicle category and conditions you want, especially automatic cars and larger family vehicles.
