Questions before booking

Useful answers, before the rental desk.

Clear educational content is also the strongest organic-search advantage for this project.

Can I rent a car in Spain with a debit card?

Yes, with some suppliers and tariffs, but the rules differ by company, pickup location, vehicle group and coverage. Read our car rental in Spain with a debit card guide before booking.

Can I rent a car in Spain without a credit card?

Yes, selected suppliers publish debit-card options. Read our car rental in Spain without a credit card guide to compare pickup and deposit rules.

How can I find a low-deposit car rental in Spain?

Compare the security deposit separately from the headline rental price. Our low-deposit car rental Spain guide explains current no-deposit and reduced-deposit options.

Is the security deposit included in the price?

No. A deposit is normally a temporary card hold. It should always be displayed separately from the rental cost so travellers understand the available credit they need.

Does MySpanishCar process my payment?

No. This site is designed as a comparison and affiliate platform. The selected partner processes the booking, payment, changes and cancellations.

Are the prices on this prototype live?

No. The current cards are design examples. Live inventory requires an approved affiliate widget, deep links or API integration.
