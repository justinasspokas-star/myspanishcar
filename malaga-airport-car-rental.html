In-terminal desk or shuttle: which is better?

01

Inside the terminal

Usually the simplest option after a flight because you can walk to the desk without waiting for a minibus. That convenience does not automatically mean the total rental cost or deposit will be lower.

02

Courtesy shuttle

Off-airport operators can be competitive, but allow extra collection and return time. Save the meeting-point instructions before you fly and check the final shuttle time if your flight lands late.

What to compare before choosing the cheapest offer

A headline price is only one part of the rental. For an international traveller, these conditions can matter more at the desk:

  • Security deposit: the refundable card hold and the available credit you need.
  • Credit vs debit card: confirm whether your exact card type is accepted for the selected vehicle and rate.
  • Excess: the amount you may remain responsible for if the vehicle is damaged or stolen.
  • Fuel policy: full-to-full is easy to compare, but always read the supplier wording.
  • Pickup type: terminal desk, parking collection or courtesy shuttle.
  • Late arrival: check office hours and any out-of-hours process before booking an evening flight.

Do you actually need a rental car in Málaga?

If you are staying only in central Málaga, a car is not essential for every trip. Aena says the C1 train reaches Málaga Centro in approximately 12 minutes and Málaga María Zambrano station in about 8 minutes. Renting becomes more useful when your itinerary includes several coastal resorts, inland towns or accommodation away from convenient rail and bus routes.

For a road-based Costa del Sol itinerary, consider your parking arrangements before booking. A smaller car can be easier in older urban areas, while a larger vehicle may make sense for families with luggage or longer multi-stop trips.

Málaga Airport FAQ

Questions travellers ask before booking

Which car rental companies are inside Málaga Airport?

Aena currently lists Avis/Budget, Europcar, Proa Rent a Car, Goldcar, Hertz/Firefly, Record Go, Sixt and OK Rent a Car in the basement of Terminal 2. Reconfirm on your booking voucher because airport arrangements can change.

Are there off-airport rental companies at AGP?

Yes. Centauro and Wiber are examples of nearby operators using courtesy shuttle collection. Their official Málaga pages provide current meeting-point instructions and office hours.

Can I use a debit card?

Possibly, but do not assume that “pay online with debit” means the same card is accepted for the security deposit at collection. Check the supplier’s card rules for your exact rate and vehicle category.

Is the deposit included in the rental price?

No. A security deposit is normally a temporary card hold rather than part of the rental charge. MySpanishCar treats the rental cost and refundable deposit as separate figures.

Can I drive a Málaga rental car to Gibraltar or Portugal?

Cross-border permission depends on the rental company and rate. Some suppliers require prior authorisation or charge a cross-border fee, so confirm the countries allowed before booking.

How far is Málaga Airport from the city centre?

Aena describes the airport as roughly 8 km from central Málaga. Its C1 train service takes approximately 12 minutes to Málaga Centro.
