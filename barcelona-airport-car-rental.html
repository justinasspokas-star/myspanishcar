Barcelona city stay or Catalonia road trip?

A rental car is not essential for a Barcelona-only city break, where public transport is extensive and parking can add cost. It becomes more valuable when your itinerary continues to the Costa Brava, Montserrat, wine regions, inland Catalonia or the Pyrenees.

If you do rent from the airport, terminal accuracy matters. A booking that requires a different terminal or shuttle meeting point can add unnecessary time after a flight.

Four checks before you collect the keys

  1. 1
    Match your booking to T1 or T2

    Do not rely on the airport name alone. Save the terminal-specific desk or shuttle instructions.

  2. 2
    Avoid keeping a car unused in central Barcelona

    If your first days are city-only, compare collecting the car later with paying city parking.

  3. 3
    Check toll and cross-border plans

    If your route includes France or Andorra, confirm cross-border permission and any fees in advance.

  4. 4
    Leave more time for airport return

    Barcelona Airport is large; wrong-terminal returns can cause stress before departure.

Barcelona Airport FAQ

Questions travellers ask before booking

Which car rental companies are inside Barcelona Airport?

Aena currently lists major operators including Avis, Enterprise, Europcar, Goldcar, Hertz/Thrifty, OK Mobility, Sixt and Record Go across T1 and T2.

Are car rental desks in both T1 and T2?

Yes. Aena publishes separate car-hire listings for T1 and T2. Always check the terminal shown on your booking voucher.

Does Centauro have an airport shuttle in Barcelona?

Yes. Centauro’s current Barcelona Airport instructions describe shuttle collection from both T1 and T2 to its nearby office.

Do I need a car in Barcelona city?

Usually not for a city-only trip. A rental is more useful if you are continuing to the Costa Brava, Montserrat, inland Catalonia or the Pyrenees.

Can I drive a Barcelona rental car into France or Andorra?

Possibly, but cross-border permission depends on the supplier and rate. Check the allowed countries and any fees before booking.
