Editorial methodology

How we compare car-rental options in Spain

Our goal is not to rank every rental company with a single score. Instead, we separate the parts of a rental that can materially change the traveller’s experience: pickup location, payment card, security deposit, fuel policy, vehicle restrictions and the final partner booking terms.

  1. 1. Start with primary sources

    Airport presence is checked against airport-operator information such as Aena. Card, deposit and rental rules are checked against the rental company’s published terms or help centre where possible.

  2. 2. Separate facts from interpretation

    We distinguish “this company has a desk in Terminal 1” from editorial advice such as “terminal pickup may save time”.

  3. 3. Check the exact booking variables

    Payment rules can vary by country, station, vehicle group, tariff and coverage. We avoid presenting a general rule as a guarantee for every booking.

  4. 4. Never invent live prices

    If we do not have authorised live availability or an API, we use “check prices” or “view deal” rather than publishing a fabricated current price.

  5. 5. Keep affiliate links separate

    Affiliate links are labelled and use sponsored attributes. A commission relationship does not replace our source checks.

  6. 6. Re-review pages

    High-intent guides are periodically reviewed, and the visible “last reviewed” date is updated when the content itself has been checked.

What we compare

Pickup

Terminal desk, car park, shuttle or off-airport location.

Payment

Credit/debit-card requirements, physical card and driver-name rules.

Deposit

Security hold or charge, where the provider publishes it.

Rental terms

Fuel, mileage, vehicle category, age and coverage conditions.

Sources and uncertainty

Where an official source conflicts with a generic marketing page, we prefer the more specific rental terms for the relevant country or station. If a condition remains unclear, we say that it must be confirmed rather than converting uncertainty into a hard claim.

Our current affiliate integration

Centauro Spain is currently an approved affiliate partner through CJ. Partner links are tracked for attribution, but editorial pages may also discuss providers with no commercial relationship.
