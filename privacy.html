Privacy Policy

MySpanishCar is a car-rental comparison and affiliate website for international travellers visiting Spain.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller and website operator is Justinas Spokas, operating the MySpanishCar brand at https://myspanishcar.com. Privacy enquiries can be sent to justinasspokas@gmail.com.

Contact information: additional postal and business-identification details will be published where required before commercial processing that legally requires them begins.

2. Data we may process

  • Technical information required to deliver the website.
  • Your cookie-consent choice, stored locally in your browser.
  • Aggregated website-usage information through Google Analytics, only after consent.
  • Information you voluntarily send by email.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Technical data is processed to provide and secure the website. Optional analytics is processed with your consent. Email enquiries are processed to answer your request and maintain necessary correspondence.

4. Affiliate partners

When you follow a partner link, you leave MySpanishCar. The partner becomes responsible for the data used to search, book, pay for or manage a vehicle rental. Review the partner’s privacy information before booking.

5. International processing

Google Analytics may involve processing by Google entities and infrastructure outside your country. Analytics remains disabled until you consent. Refer to Google’s privacy information for details of its safeguards.

6. Retention

Consent choices are kept for up to 180 days. Analytics retention is controlled in the Google Analytics property. Email correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to answer the enquiry and meet applicable obligations.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and may withdraw consent at any time. Contact justinasspokas@gmail.com. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority.
