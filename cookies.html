Cookie Policy

This policy explains how MySpanishCar uses cookies and similar browser storage on myspanishcar.com.

1. What we use

The website uses essential browser storage to remember your privacy choice. Google Analytics is optional and only loads after you allow analytics in the cookie banner or settings panel.

2. Google Analytics

When allowed, Google Analytics may process visit statistics, device and browser information, approximate location and interactions with the website. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation remain disabled in our current configuration.

3. Your choice

You may accept analytics, reject non-essential cookies or configure your choice. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using MySpanishCar. You can reopen the panel through at any time.

4. Browser controls

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential browser storage may cause the website to ask for your choice again.
