Deposit, excess and rental price are three different numbers

€

Rental price

What the rental itself costs, including the selected tariff and extras.

Hold

Security deposit

An amount held, charged or preauthorised as security for contractual obligations.

Risk

Damage excess

The amount of financial responsibility that can apply to damage under the chosen cover.

Cover

Protection package

Extra coverage can reduce or remove an excess/deposit but raises the upfront rental price.

How to compare low-deposit rentals properly

  • Find the security deposit for the exact vehicle group
  • Check whether the damage excess is separate
  • Confirm whether coverage removes the deposit or only the excess
  • Check whether fuel has its own preauthorisation
  • Compare the total rental cost after adding required coverage
  • Check how long the supplier says release/refund can take
  • Bring a card with sufficient available funds even on low-deposit tariffs

No-deposit options currently published by suppliers

Wiber publishes All Inclusive and SmartCar tariffs with no deposit or excess. Centauro advertises a Premium option with no deposit and no excess, while explicitly noting that exceptions can apply. These products can be attractive if keeping card liquidity available matters more than getting the lowest headline rental price.

When a higher rental price can still be better value

Suppose one tariff costs €90 with a substantial deposit and another costs €125 with a no-deposit protection package. The second is €35 more expensive, but it may fit a traveller with a lower card limit or limited available cash much better. This is why MySpanishCar separates price from deposit.

Frequently asked questions

Low deposit car rental Spain FAQ

Can I rent a car in Spain with a low deposit?

Yes. Deposit amounts vary by supplier, tariff, coverage and vehicle category. Some suppliers offer premium or all-inclusive products that reduce or remove certain deposits.

Which car rental companies in Spain offer no-deposit options?

Wiber advertises All Inclusive and SmartCar options with no deposit or excess. Centauro advertises a Premium option with no deposit/no excess, subject to stated exceptions and booking conditions.

Does full coverage always remove every deposit?

No. Coverage can remove an excess or security hold under certain products, but exceptions may remain. For example, fuel, tolls, special vehicle groups or other guarantees may be treated separately.

Is a low-deposit rental always cheaper overall?

Not necessarily. A low-deposit or no-deposit tariff can cost more upfront because extra coverage is included. Compare total rental cost, deposit and coverage together.

How long can a deposit take to return?

Timing varies by supplier and bank. Some companies state that a preauthorisation can take days or weeks to disappear from the account after the rental closes.
