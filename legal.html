MySpanishCar provides independent travel information and is being developed as a comparison and affiliate website for vehicle-rental offers in Spain. MySpanishCar does not own rental vehicles and does not currently process rental payments or bookings. A vehicle-rental contract is formed directly with the selected third-party rental company, broker or booking partner, subject to that party’s prices, availability and terms.
