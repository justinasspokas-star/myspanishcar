Why a rental car works particularly well in Tenerife

Tenerife packs beaches, volcanic scenery, high-altitude roads and very different microclimates into one island. A car makes it easier to combine the south coast with Teide, the north and smaller villages on your own schedule.

The trade-off is terrain. The fastest-looking route on a map can include steep gradients, hairpins or slower mountain sections, so build extra time into day trips and avoid choosing a vehicle purely on headline price.

Four checks before you collect the keys

  1. 1
    Choose a car you are comfortable driving uphill

    A compact car is easy to park, but make sure the engine and transmission suit your route and luggage load.

  2. 2
    Do not underestimate altitude and weather changes

    Conditions around Teide can be very different from the coast. Check local road and weather information before mountain trips.

  3. 3
    Record fuel and body condition at pickup

    Island rentals can involve a lot of driving; document the car before leaving the airport.

  4. 4
    Save the exact return point

    Large airport rental areas can have different vehicle-return lanes or exterior zones.

Tenerife South Airport FAQ

Questions travellers ask before booking

Which companies rent cars at Tenerife South Airport?

Aena currently lists AutoReisen, Cicar, Europcar, Goldcar, Hertz, Record Go and TopCar across terminal and exterior rental areas.

Do I need a car in Tenerife?

Not every visitor needs one, but a car is particularly useful if you want to explore beyond one resort and visit Teide, the north coast or several beaches.

Can I rent with a debit card at Tenerife South?

It depends on the supplier and rate. Check the deposit and accepted-card rules for the exact vehicle category before booking.

Is driving in Tenerife difficult?

Main roads are straightforward, but mountain and village roads can be steep, narrow and winding. Choose a vehicle you feel confident driving.

Does Centauro serve Tenerife South?

Centauro markets rentals for Tenerife South Airport. Because Aena does not list it among the terminal desks shown on its car-hire page, follow Centauro’s current booking instructions for pickup.
